Block Glide – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 November 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Block Glide (“the game”), made by Muhammad Zafar Hayat (“we”, “us”), collects, how it is used and who it is shared with.

1. Information stored on your device

Your game progress (levels reached, stars, coins, helpers and settings such as sound) is saved only on your device. We do not receive it and you do not need an account. Deleting the game deletes this information.

2. Information we collect

We do not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos or location, and the game has no sign-in or chat.

To give out daily rewards fairly, the game asks our server for today's date. Like any web request, this reaches our server with your device's IP address. We use it only to answer the request and do not store it with any other information about you.

3. Advertising

The game is free and shows ads, provided by Google AdMob. Some ads you choose to watch for a reward (for example extra coins); others may appear between levels. To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device type, operating system, app usage and approximate location (from the IP address). Depending on where you live, you may be asked for consent to personalised ads, and you can change that choice in the game's settings.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your device's settings (on Android: Settings → Google → Ads). How Google uses this information is explained at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

4. In-app purchases

You can buy coins, bundles and “Remove ads” in the game. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your card or payment details; we only receive confirmation of what was bought, so the game can add it.

5. Who information is shared with

  • Google (AdMob) – to show and measure ads, as described above.
  • Google Play – to process purchases.

We do not sell your personal information.

6. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Security and retention

Information sent between the game and our server is encrypted (HTTPS). We keep no personal information beyond what is described above. Information collected by Google is kept according to Google's own policies.

8. Your choices and rights

You can turn off personalised ads in the game or in your device settings, and delete all game data by uninstalling the game or using “Start over” in the game's settings. Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information about you; contact us to use them.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and its effective date. Important changes will also be shown in the game.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy or your information: pentainnov8systems@gmail.com
